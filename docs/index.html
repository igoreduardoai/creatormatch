CreatorMatch

Popularity isn't affinity with your brand. Name a brand and the creators (podcasts) you are considering. CreatorMatch compares a popularity sort with a sort by what the brand's audience actually loves, from the Qloo taste graph, and explains its least obvious pick with evidence.

Open source (MIT): github.com/igoreduardoai/creatormatch. Qloo Agentic Hackathon.
